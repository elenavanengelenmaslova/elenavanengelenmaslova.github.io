## The Forward-Deployed Model

A focused, short-term injection of senior engineering into your team. I embed directly in your existing workflow — your repos, your standups, your tooling — and ramp up fast, so I'm contributing real work instead of sitting through a month of onboarding. We agree on a specific, well-scoped outcome up front; I build it, ship it, and make sure your engineers can own and extend it long after the engagement ends.

You get the result, your team keeps the knowledge, and the engagement closes on a clear deliverable — no lingering dependency, no open-ended retainer.

**Format:** up to two weeks full-time, or longer part-time when it fits better around your team's schedule.

**Pricing:** per hour when scope is evolving and you want flexible capacity, or a finished, fixed-price project when the outcome is well-defined and you want cost certainty up front.

**Availability:** available now.
